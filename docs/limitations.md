# Limitations

## 1. Text-Based Input

The proposed system processes written radiology reports only. Medical images are not directly analysed or used as model inputs.

## 2. Knowledge Base Verification

The terminology knowledge base used in the retrieval experiments has not undergone expert verification. Therefore, the retrieved explanations may contain incomplete or imperfect information.

## 3. Automated Evaluation

The generated summaries are evaluated using automatic text-generation, semantic similarity and readability measures. These metrics cannot fully determine whether a summary is clinically appropriate or understandable to every patient.

## 4. Hallucination Estimation

NLI-based evaluation is used as an automated method for estimating potential hallucinations. It is a proxy measure and does not replace expert review.

## 5. No Clinical Validation

Clinical evaluation by radiologists or other qualified healthcare professionals has not been conducted as part of the reported experiments. The system should not be considered clinically validated.

## 6. Model Limitations

The language models may omit important information, generate unsupported statements or simplify medical terminology incorrectly. Further evaluation and improvement are required before practical clinical use.
